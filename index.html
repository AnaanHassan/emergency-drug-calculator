Resus Dose Calculator

Check before you give. Paediatric resuscitation doses follow APLS (Resuscitation Council UK 2021) and adult resuscitation doses follow AHA ACLS. Other drugs follow BNF/BNFc, with RCH Melbourne for agitation. Confirm against your local protocol, the product concentration in your hand, renal/hepatic function and the clinical picture. Neonates (<1 month) need neonatal references and are out of scope.

Estimated weight uses the APLS formulas: 0–12 months (0.5 × months) + 4; 1–5 years (2 × age) + 8; 6–12 years (3 × age) + 7. Weigh the child when you can. Card colours follow the ISO 26825 user-applied syringe label scheme.
